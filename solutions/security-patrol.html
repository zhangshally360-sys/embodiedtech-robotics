Solution

Security Patrol & Inspection Solution

End-to-end quadruped robot patrol system — hardware, AI software platform, and deployment services for public security, industrial parks, and critical infrastructure.

Challenges We Solve

Complex Terrain

Stairs, slopes, debris, confined spaces — traditional wheeled robots and fixed cameras fail. Quadruped robots handle all terrain.

24/7 Operation

Autonomous patrol around the clock with auto-charging. No breaks, no fatigue, consistent coverage every shift.

Manpower Shortage

Reduce patrol headcount by 50%+ while improving coverage quality. One operator manages a fleet of robots.

Situational Awareness

Real-time video, thermal imaging, gas detection, acoustic sensing — all data feeds into a unified command platform.
