Unitree G1 Humanoid Robot

The G1 is Unitree's compact humanoid robot — standing ~1.32m tall and weighing ~35kg. With 23 degrees of freedom, 90N.m knee torque, and 2kg arm load capacity, the G1 is ideal for research, education, and light service applications.

Available in Base and multiple Edu versions with Nvidia Jetson Orin 100 Tops computing, upgraded 120N.m knee torque, 3kg arm load, and optional dexterous hands (Dex3-1 three-finger or Revo 2 five-finger). The advanced versions add 3-DOF waist and 7-DOF arms for a total of 29-43 DOF.
