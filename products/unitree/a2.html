Unitree A2 / A2S Robot Dog

The A2 and A2S are Unitree's advanced aerial manipulation quadrupeds featuring limb control — a 4-DOF arm mounted on the robot dog for active manipulation tasks. The 11kg air load capacity enables carrying and deploying sensors and tools in elevated positions.

The A2S sensor version adds comprehensive perception capabilities for inspection tasks requiring environmental awareness.
