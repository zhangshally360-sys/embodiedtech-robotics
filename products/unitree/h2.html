Unitree H2 Large Humanoid Robot

The H2 is Unitree's adult-size humanoid robot platform designed for advanced manipulation research. With support for multiple dexterous hand types including the 16-DOF Dex5-1 five-finger hand, the H2 pushes the boundaries of humanoid dexterity.

Equipped with Nvidia Jetson AGX Thor (2070 Tops) or AGX Orin (275 Tops) computing modules, Intel RealSense D455 depth camera in the head, and support for 3D LiDAR integration.
