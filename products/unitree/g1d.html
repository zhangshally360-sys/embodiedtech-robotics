Unitree G1-D Data Collection Robot

The G1-D is a wheeled data collection and AI reasoning platform combining a humanoid upper body with a stable wheeled base. Designed for AI model training, data acquisition, and embodied intelligence research in real-world environments.

Available in Standard (50kg, 2hr) and Flagship (80kg, wheeled base + 6hr) editions with Nvidia Jetson Orin 100 Tops, 4-microphone array, and multi-sensor perception suite.
