Unitree GO2 Robot Dog

The GO2 is Unitree's most popular quadruped robot dog — compact, powerful, and packed with intelligence. Ideal for education, research, light inspection, and hobbyist applications.

Featuring 5m/s max running speed, 15kg payload capacity, 360° LiDAR, 4D ultra-wide vision, and Wi-Fi 6 + 4G connectivity. Available in Edu and Pro editions with Nvidia Jetson Orin 100 Tops computing power.
