Unitree B2 Industrial Robot Dog

The B2 is Unitree's large-scale industrial quadruped robot, purpose-built for heavy-duty inspection, patrol, and industrial missions. With 20km operating range, IP67 protection, and ability to traverse 40° slopes and 80cm obstacles, the B2 operates in the harshest conditions.

Featuring 30+kg payload capacity, 3+ hours patrol runtime, autonomous charging support, and multi-sensor payload integration — the B2 is the workhorse of industrial robotics.
