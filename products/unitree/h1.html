Unitree H1 / H1-2 Humanoid Robot

The H1 and H1-2 are Unitree's full-size humanoid robots — standing 1.76m tall with the world's highest power density. The H1 is the strongest humanoid in its class globally, with 360N.m knee torque and 3.3m/s walking speed (potential >5m/s).

The H1-2 adds 7-DOF arms, 6-DOF legs per side, 3D LiDAR, depth cameras, dual Intel Core i7 processors, and weighs ~73kg for enhanced stability and dexterity. Comes with dual hot-swappable batteries.
