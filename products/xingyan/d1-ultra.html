XingYan D1 Ultra Patrol Robot

The D1 Ultra is XingYan's small compact patrol quadruped — the only industry-grade small quadruped in the market. With 3.7m/s running, 35cm jumping, and the ability to climb 16cm stairs, it packs exceptional motion performance into a compact body.

IP54 rated, rugged and impact-resistant, with 5kg rated payload and 2-hour battery life. Equipped with reconnaissance gimbal, loudspeaker, and optional LiDAR/thermal camera for security patrol, evidence collection, and acoustic/light warning missions.
