XingYan D1 Max Patrol Robot

The D1 Max is XingYan's medium-sized quadruped patrol robot designed for professional security and industrial inspection. With exceptional terrain capability — 45° slopes, 80cm vertical obstacles, and 26cm stair climbing — it handles the toughest urban and industrial environments.

Equipped with dual-spectrum gimbal (thermal + visible), 60W high-power speaker, strobe lights, and optional gas detection & acoustic imaging payloads. Full autonomous patrol and charging supported.
