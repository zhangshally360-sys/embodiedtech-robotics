XingYan D1 Max Pro Large Patrol Robot

The D1 Max Pro is XingYan's largest and most capable quadruped — a heavy-duty robot purpose-built for the most demanding industrial inspection and emergency response missions. With 50kg walking payload (100kg static), this robot carries the heaviest mission payloads over the longest durations.

Built-in reinforcement learning locomotion control for true all-terrain adaptability. Supports fully autonomous patrol, environment perception, auto-charging, and auto-alerting. Compatible with dual-spectrum thermal gimbal, 3D LiDAR, gas sensors, and acoustic imagers. Comes with integrated cloud patrol management platform.
