About Us

Your One-Stop Robotics
Sourcing Partner

Connecting global buyers with China's best embodied robot manufacturers.

EmbodiedTech Robotics is a specialized trading and supply chain company under Xiamen Ray Spung Technology Co., Ltd., focused exclusively on embodied intelligent robots. We partner with China's top robot manufacturers — Unitree Robotics (world leader in quadrupeds & humanoids), DeepBlue Technology (AI company with 50+ world AI championships), and XingYan Intelligent Control (security patrol specialists) — to bring their cutting-edge products to global markets.

Our portfolio covers the full spectrum: robot dogs (GO2, B2, A2), humanoid robots (G1, H1, H1-2, H2), data collection & reasoning robots (G1-D), security patrol robots (D1 Ultra/Max/Max Pro), healthcare robots, companion robots, cleaning robots, and complete patrol & inspection software platforms.

Headquartered in Xiamen, China, we provide end-to-end services: product sourcing & comparison, OEM/ODM customization, certification compliance (CE, RoHS), factory audit, pre-shipment inspection, logistics (FOB/CIF/DDP), and after-sales support with 3-year standard warranty.
